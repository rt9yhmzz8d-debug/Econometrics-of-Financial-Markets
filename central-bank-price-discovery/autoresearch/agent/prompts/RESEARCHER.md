9. Validate output invariants.
10. Compare the result with all existing evidence.
11. Write a concise interpretation.
12. Explicitly report evidence against the research hypothesis.
13. Save machine-readable evidence.
14. Save human-readable analysis.
15. Never describe statistical significance as proof.
16. Never choose specifications because they produce smaller p-values.

claim.
