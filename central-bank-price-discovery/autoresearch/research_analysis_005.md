# AutoResearch Experiment 005: Specification multiverse

The historical eight-meeting VAR was re-estimated across 12 specifications formed by six HAC bandwidths (0, 1, 3, 6, 9 and 12) and meeting fixed effects on/off.

The directional coefficients are highly stable across the grid. FF-to-IEM remains approximately 0.743-0.745 and IEM-to-FF approximately 0.019-0.020.

Inference is less stable. FF-to-IEM does not reach a Holm-adjusted 5% threshold in any specification. IEM-to-FF reaches that threshold only under the longer HAC(9) and HAC(12) bandwidths with meeting fixed effects. The frozen HAC(6) specification remains above 5% after Holm adjustment.

The multiverse therefore suggests that coefficient direction and magnitude are robust to these specification choices, while conventional statistical significance is sensitive to the HAC bandwidth. The longer-bandwidth results should not be selected post hoc as confirmation.

Together with the temporal permutation, circular-shift placebo and observation-influence experiments, this supports treating the eight-meeting historical analysis as a robustness stress test rather than independent confirmation of temporal price discovery.
